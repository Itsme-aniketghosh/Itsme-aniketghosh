### 👋 About Me

Hey, I'm Aniket. I build ML systems for problems where being wrong has consequences.

I'm doing my **MS in Artificial Intelligence** at **Northeastern University** (Khoury College, 4.0 GPA). This fall I'm an **interpretability research mentee at SPAR**, building ground-truth probes for language models, and a **TA for CS 5800 Algorithms**. Over the summer I was the **sole ML engineer at Varosync**, a drug-development startup, where I built a biomedical knowledge graph over millions of molecules and trained 7–8B molecular embedding models on an 800M-molecule corpus.

Long term, I want to work on medical AI that is interpretable by design.
